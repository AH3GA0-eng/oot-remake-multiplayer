# hs-pause-tab, B (part of hs-pause-tab.steps.txt): the pause menu's Multiplayer tab and page in a joined game.
# PLUS opens the menu; R four times reaches System (no wrap: it stops there); R once more shows our page.
press PLUS
wait 240
press R
wait 90
press R
wait 90
press R
wait 90
press R
wait 120
mark system
press R
wait 120
mark page
press DDOWN
wait 60
mark page-down
press L
wait 120
mark system-back
press PLUS
wait 150
mark closed
