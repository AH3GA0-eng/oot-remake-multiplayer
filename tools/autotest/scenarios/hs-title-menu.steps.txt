# hs-title-menu (Hookshot only): the title screen's own co-op rows Host, Join and Co-op Settings (patch 0004 + the
# puppet pack's title_menu step; docs/hookshot.md "The title screen"), in BOTH games, with screenshot proof.
# Needs the kit's pack made with --title-rows (hookshot-build.ps1 since 0.11.5; the pack's coop_puppet.json says
# "title_rows": true). Both games start as the harness starts them (co-op on from the start) and stay at the title.
# Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Runtime hookshot -Scenario hs-title-menu
# Shots (s<step>-<name>-A/-B.png) and checks (proofs.txt, title-*):
#   top       the title, the cursor on Continue: seven rows in the game's own look (Continue, New Game, Options,
#             the game's fourth, then Host, Join, Co-op Settings)
#   on-host   the game's own highlight on Host (the list changed from top)
#   pressed   Hookshot's menu on the Multiplayer tab's Host page over the title (co-op came with the start: the page
#             says "Co-op is on" and how it started; a press never does nothing)
#   closed    the title again, the menu gone
# The change checks' thresholds are a first guess: the first run calibrates them (proof.py dist), then refs.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1
#! timeout 300
start A
start B
sleep 30
lines rows-A A min=1 coop: title: the title's own rows Host, Join, Co-op Settings \(buttons 4 to 6\)$
lines rows-B B min=1 coop: title: the title's own rows Host, Join, Co-op Settings \(buttons 4 to 6\)$
lines no-rows-A A max=0 coop: title: the title has \d+ buttons, not the puppet pack's
lines no-rows-B B max=0 coop: title: the title has \d+ buttons, not the puppet pack's
lines no-text-A A max=0 their texts could not be set
lines no-text-B B max=0 their texts could not be set
shot top
play A hs-title-menu.down.txt B hs-title-menu.down.txt
shot on-host
see title-on-host-A title-on-host-B
lines focus-A A min=1 coop: title: the title's focus is on button 4$
lines focus-B B min=1 coop: title: the title's focus is on button 4$
play A hs-title-menu.join.txt B hs-title-menu.join.txt
shot on-join
lines join-A A min=1 coop: title: the title's focus is on button 5$
lines join-B B min=1 coop: title: the title's focus is on button 5$
play A hs-title-menu.up.txt B hs-title-menu.up.txt
play A hs-title-menu.press.txt B hs-title-menu.press.txt
shot pressed
see title-pressed-A title-pressed-B
lines pressed-A A min=1 coop: title: the title's row 0100 pressed
lines pressed-B B min=1 coop: title: the title's row 0100 pressed
play A hs-title-menu.close.txt B hs-title-menu.close.txt
shot closed
see title-closed-A title-closed-B
