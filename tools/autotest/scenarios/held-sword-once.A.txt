# held-sword-once: does the puppet's sword need the local sword drawn once, or out now? B draws its own
# sword at the start and puts it away (held-sword-once.B.txt); only then A walks up and draws. B's shots
# "a-sword"/"a-sword2" show whether the puppet's blade is drawn while B's own sword is put away.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_ANIM=1
mark start
stick 0 0.6 100
stick 0.7 0 30
stick 0 -0.4 14
wait 900
mark before
press Y 8
wait 60
mark a-sword
wait 60
mark a-sword2
hold ZL 60
mark a-sword-z
press B 12
wait 120
mark end
