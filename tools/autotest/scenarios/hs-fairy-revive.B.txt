# hs-fairy-revive, B: lands in Hyrule Field in front of Kakariko with a fairy in bottle A, at half a heart if the
# game takes PlayerStatus.Life (else its save's hearts), and stands at 213 14 while A's sword takes its life
# (see hs-fairy-revive.A.txt). The game's own death then uses the fairy.
mark start
warp HyruleField_Child HyruleField_FrontOfKakarikoVillage
wait 1200
mark title
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 300
press A
wait 2400
mark field
setflag PouchData.BottleA.StockNum 1
wait 30
setflag PlayerStatus.AssignableItem.BottleInfo.BottleA.IsValid 1
wait 30
setenum PlayerStatus.AssignableItem.BottleInfo.BottleA.BottleItem Fairy
wait 30
setflag PlayerStatus.Life 2
wait 60
goto 213.0 14.0 0.4 600
toward 215.0 14.0 6     # face A
mark standing
wait 1800
mark mid
wait 1800
mark after-swings
# Fallback when PvP's sword did not reach (no 'coop: pvp: hit' lines): the test kill of the nearest Player_Child
# with a life (our own Link, through the game's own damage), if the objects walk lists it; else 'none here'.
kill Player_Child
wait 1500
mark end
