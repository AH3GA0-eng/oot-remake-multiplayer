# hs-fairy-revive (Hookshot, branch down-spectate, row 113 research; no PASS/FAIL of the game, a measurement):
# how long the game's own bottled-fairy revive keeps Link at no life, so down but not out's kDyingUs (8 s, the
# fallback before a Link at 0 counts as out) is known to be longer. A hosts (joins first), turns down but not out
# off (so B's death is the game's own) and PvP on, then swings its sword at B (half a heart a hit) in Hyrule Field
# in front of Kakariko (the sword is read from the grass), as pvp.A.txt does. B carries a fairy in bottle A and
# starts at half a heart if the game takes PlayerStatus.Life (hs-fairy-revive.B.txt).
# Read: B's "coop: objects: our Link's life N of M" lines (LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_OBJECTS=1): the first "life 0 of" and
# the next "life N of" (N > 0) are the revive; the offline job timestamps them as they are written. Also B's
# "coop: pvp: hit from P1" lines and the puppets' actor names ("coop: objects: + CoopPuppet_").
# Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Runtime hookshot -Lane 1 -Scenario hs-fairy-revive -Server <protocol 12 server>

#! switches LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_OBJECTS=1,LICHII_COOP_CHEATS=1
#! timeout 480
